ABOUT ANSH

A Little About Me

Hi, I'm Ansh — a science educator who enjoys making complicated concepts easy to understand.

With over three years of teaching experience, I've worked with students across different countries and academic curricula, including IB, Cambridge, IGCSE, CBSE and State Boards, along with rigorous JEE and NEET preparation.

My academic journey includes research experience at IIT Indore, which helped me explore science beyond textbooks and examinations.

Through Concepts by Ansh, I aim to create a learning environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, understanding concepts and building lasting confidence.
